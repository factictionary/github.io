❓ Contact FAQs

I purchased a Payhip digital download — where is my file?

Payhip emails your download link automatically after payment. Check your Spam/Junk folder. If you still cannot find it, email us with your order email address and we will resend the link.

Are my uploaded files stored on your servers?

No! All our tools (PDF, Image, Video, Audio) operate 100% client-side in your browser memory via WebAssembly. Zero files are uploaded to any server.

Can I suggest a new web tool or feature?

Yes! We welcome suggestions for useful calculators, converters, or developer tools. Send us an email with your idea.

2. 25+ Tambola Winning Patterns (Dividends) for Kitty Parties & Festivals

Instead of playing only the basic 5 prizes (Early 5, Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Full House), add 4–6 of these creative dividends to keep every player in the game until the end:

Dividend Name How to Win (Claim Rule) Difficulty
Early Five (Jaldi 5) First player to strike off any 5 numbers on their ticket. Easy (Fastest)
Four Corners 1st and 5th numbers of the Top Row + 1st and 5th numbers of the Bottom Row (4 numbers total). Easy
Queen's Corner / King's Corner All 4 corner numbers PLUS the central (3rd) number of the middle row (5 numbers total). Medium
Bamboo (Central Pillar) The 3rd (middle) number of the Top, Middle, and Bottom rows (3 numbers total). Easy
Temperature / BP (High-Low) The smallest number on the ticket + the largest number on the ticket (2 numbers total). Very Easy
Jawani & Budhapa (Youth & Old Age) Jawani = all numbers from 1 to 45 on your ticket; Budhapa = all numbers from 46 to 90 on your ticket. Medium-Hard
Pyramid 3rd number of Top Row + 2nd & 4th numbers of Middle Row + 1st, 3rd & 5th numbers of Bottom Row. Medium
Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Breakfast = Columns 1–3 (1–29); Lunch = Columns 4–6 (30–59); Dinner = Columns 7–9 (60–90). Medium
Unlucky 1 (Booby Prize) Awarded to the player who still has 0 numbers struck when the first line or Jaldi 5 is claimed! Fun Bonus
Half Sheet / Full Sheet Bonus At least 2 numbers struck on each of 3 consecutive tickets (Half Sheet) or 6 tickets (Full Sheet). Group / Club

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What happens if a player calls "Bogey" (false claim) in Tambola?

If a player claims a line or Full House and the host's board verification shows even one number has not yet been called, that ticket is declared a Bogey and is disqualified from claiming further prizes in that round.

Can two players split a Tambola prize?

Yes! If two players complete Top Line or Full House on the exact same called number and both shout "Yes / Housie" before the next number is drawn, the prize dividend is split equally between them.

📖 Self-Publishing Masterclass

The Complete Amazon KDP Publishing Handbook

⏱ 12 Min Read 📅 Updated for 2026 ✍️ Factictionary Editorial

Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) has revolutionized the publishing industry, empowering independent authors to reach millions of readers worldwide without traditional publishing gatekeepers. This handbook covers the end-to-end self-publishing workflow—from trim sizes and cover math to backend keyword optimization and royalty pricing strategies.

1. Trim Sizes & Page Layout Preparation

Selecting the right trim size is the foundational step when formatting your book for print. KDP supports standard industry sizes including 5" x 8", 5.5" x 8.5", and the popular 6" x 9" format for fiction and non-fiction.

  • Standard Fiction & Non-Fiction: 6" x 9" (15.24 x 22.86 cm)
  • Compact Novels & Memoirs: 5.5" x 8.5" or 5" x 8"
  • Workbooks & Low-Content Books: 8.5" x 11" (21.59 x 27.94 cm)
💡 Bleed vs. No Bleed: If images or design elements extend to the edge of the page, select "Bleed" and add 0.125" (3.2 mm) to your width and 0.25" (6.4 mm) to your height.

2. Cover Design Math & Spine Width Calculations

A major cause of KDP interior and cover upload errors is inaccurate spine width calculation. The total cover width must accommodate the front cover, back cover, spine width, and 0.125" outer bleed on all sides.

Spine Width Formula (Black & White Paper):
Spine Width = Page Count × 0.002252 inches (0.0572 mm)

For example, a 200-page black-and-white paperback has a spine width of 200 × 0.002252 = 0.4504 inches. Adding this to front and back 6" covers plus bleed gives a total canvas width of 12.700 inches.

3. Royalty Calculations & List Price Strategy

KDP offers a 60% royalty rate for print books sold on Amazon marketplaces (40% for Expanded Distribution). Your net profit per sale is calculated as:

Net Royalty = (List Price × 60%) − Printing Cost

For eBooks, KDP offers a 70% royalty rate for titles priced between $2.99 and $9.99, and 35% for titles below $2.99 or above $9.99.

4. Mastering KDP 7 Backend Keywords & Categories

KDP allows authors to enter up to 7 backend keyword phrases (up to 50 characters each). Avoid repeating words from your title or author name, as Amazon indexes those automatically.

  • Combine multi-word search phrases (e.g. sci-fi space opera romance for young adults)
  • Use real search terms readers type into the Amazon search bar
  • Avoid forbidden terms like "bestseller", "free", or "discount"

5. Launch Marketing & Post-Publication Checklist

Once your book is live, optimize your Amazon Author Central profile, set up A+ Content with visual mockups, and gather initial reviews through advance reader copies (ARCs).

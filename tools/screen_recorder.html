How to Record Screen, Webcam, or Whiteboard Online Without Watermark

  1. Select your recording mode: Screen / Tab (Desktop), Camera / Webcam (Desktop & Mobile), or Whiteboard + Voice (Desktop & Mobile).
  2. Choose whether to include Microphone Audio narration and/or System Audio.
  3. Click Start Recording. You can pause and resume at any time.
  4. Click Stop Recording when finished to preview your video immediately and click Download Video File to save it locally.

How Browser-Based Screen & Webcam Recording Works (MediaStream API)

Record software tutorials, bug reproduction clips, gameplay walkthroughs, or slide presentations directly inside Chrome, Edge, or Firefox without installing bloated desktop screen recorders or browser extensions.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Click "Start Recording" and select whether to capture your Entire Screen, a specific Application Window, or a single Browser Tab via your browser's native permission prompt.
  2. Optionally enable system audio or microphone narration for tutorial voiceovers.
  3. When finished, click "Stop Recording" to compile the WebM/MP4 video stream locally in RAM and save it straight to your device.

Why Open & Convert DOCX Files Client-Side?

Word documents frequently contain sensitive employment contracts, academic manuscripts, legal briefs, or resumes with personal contact information. Cloud-based document viewers upload your `.docx` file to remote storage buckets to render a preview. By processing your document directly inside your browser, Factictionary renders your pages in local memory with zero network transmission.

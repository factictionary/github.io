Instant In-Browser Microsoft Word (.DOCX) Document Reader

Select any .docx file above to view its formatted headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, and embedded images right here in your browser tab.

Unlike pasting directly from Microsoft Word into a CMS—which pollutes your code with thousands of inline mso-style attributes—Mammoth.js parses the underlying OpenXML structure (`word/document.xml`) and outputs clean, semantic HTML5 tags (`<h1>`, `<p>`, `<ul>`, `<table>`) ready for blogs, eBooks, and documentation sites.

Why Open & Convert DOCX Files Client-Side?

Word documents frequently contain sensitive employment contracts, academic manuscripts, legal briefs, or resumes with personal contact information. Cloud-based document viewers upload your `.docx` file to remote storage buckets to render a preview. By executing Mammoth.js inside your browser's local JavaScript engine, Factictionary unpacks the OpenXML container in RAM with zero network transmission.

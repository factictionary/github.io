How Our Backtracking Sudoku Solver & Logic Trainer Works

Every valid 9×9 Sudoku puzzle is a constraint-satisfaction problem requiring digits 1 through 9 to appear exactly once in each horizontal row, each vertical column, and each of the nine 3×3 sub-boxes. Our in-browser solver uses a recursive depth-first backtracking algorithm with constraint propagation to verify and solve any valid 9×9 configuration in under 5 milliseconds.

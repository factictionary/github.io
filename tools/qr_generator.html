How to Generate High-Contrast, Scannable QR Codes Locally

Quick Response (QR) two-dimensional matrix barcodes encode URLs, UPI payment strings, Wi-Fi credentials, and vCard contact details using Reed-Solomon error correction so smartphone cameras can scan them even if partially smudged.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter your destination URL, text message, Wi-Fi SSID string, or contact payload in the input field above.
  2. Customize the pixel resolution and foreground/background contrast ratio (always keep a dark foreground against a light background for reliable camera scanning).
  3. Download the crisp PNG QR code for print flyers, book back covers, tuition receipts, or product packaging.

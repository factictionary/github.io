How Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) Work

Over 90% of Fortune 500 companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter candidate resumes before a human recruiter ever sees them. These systems parse your resume text and calculate a keyword frequency match score against the job description.

By optimizing your resume for missing technical skills, action verbs, and certifications highlighted above, you dramatically increase your interview callback rate.

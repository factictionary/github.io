Text-to-Speech (TTS) Voice Reader

Read text aloud using natural browser voices with customizable pitch, speed rate, and zero character limits.

How Client-Side Browser Text-to-Speech (Web Speech API) Works

Factictionary's Text-to-Speech synthesizer utilizes your operating system's native HTML5 SpeechSynthesis API to convert written scripts, study notes, and articles into spoken audio with zero cloud uploads and zero character limits.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Paste or type your manuscript, speech draft, or foreign-language vocabulary list into the text area above.
  2. Select your preferred native system voice and language locale (such as English US/UK/IN, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, or Japanese).
  3. Adjust the Speed Rate (0.5x for language shadowing up to 2.0x for rapid proofreading) and Pitch Tone slider.
  4. Click "Read Aloud Now" to listen immediately—authors often use this to catch awkward phrasing and typos that visual reading misses.

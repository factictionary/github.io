How to Merge PDFs Online Safely

  1. Upload your PDF files by dropping them into the dropzone above or clicking Browse Files.
  2. Drag and drop the file cards up or down to set your desired page sequence.
  3. Optionally select rotation angles (90°, 180°, 270°) or page reversal per document.
  4. Click Merge PDFs Now to combine all files locally using WebAssembly and PDF-lib.
  5. Click Download Merged PDF to save the final compiled document directly to your device.

Why Client-Side Privacy Matters

Most online PDF mergers require you to upload your sensitive financial statements, contracts, or personal tax documents to cloud servers. Factictionary processes all PDF files locally inside your browser using WebAssembly. Your files are never transmitted across the network, ensuring complete confidentiality.

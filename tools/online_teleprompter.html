How to Read a Teleprompter Without Viewers Noticing Eye Movement

Whether you are recording a 60-second Instagram Reel, a YouTube tutorial, or delivering a Zoom keynote, reading wide paragraphs across a laptop monitor creates a telltale horizontal "windshield-wiper" eye motion. Follow these three broadcast studio adjustments to eliminate eye darting:

  1. Use the "Auto-Break into 5-Word Breath Lines" Button: Narrowing each line to 4–6 words allows your foveal vision to capture the entire phrase in a single center fixation without sweeping left to right.
  2. Read Exclusively Inside the Top Eye-Line Band: Never chase words down to the bottom of the window. Adjust the Speed slider so the text rises into the blue highlight band at your natural 135–150 Words Per Minute speaking cadence.
  3. Step Back 3 Feet (or Use Our Mobile App): Increasing the distance between your eyes and the lens cuts the angular offset in half. If you shoot vertically on a smartphone, install Teleprompter & AI Speech Coach to overlay your script right beside the top selfie camera.

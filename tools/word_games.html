How Daily Word Puzzles & Anagrams Improve Lexical Recall

Playing 5-letter word deduction grids (Wordle-style), unscrambling anagrams, and testing synonym accuracy activates both the phonological loop and orthographic processing centers of the brain. Unlike passive reading, active letter-elimination puzzles train you to recognize consonant clusters, vowel distribution patterns, and prefix/suffix structures under time constraints.

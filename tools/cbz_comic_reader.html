What Is a .CBZ (Comic Book ZIP) File & How Does In-Browser Reading Work?

A CBZ (Comic Book ZIP) file is an industry-standard archive containing sequentially numbered image panels (`.jpg`, `.png`, or `.webp`) used for digital comic books, indie graphic novels, and manga chapters. Our browser reader uses JSZip to sort image entries naturally by filename (`page_001.jpg`, `page_002.jpg`, etc.) and generates zero-latency `Blob` URLs inside your device RAM.

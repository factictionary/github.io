Troubleshooting Beta Access

Why does Google Play say "App not available" when I tap Step 2?

Make sure you tapped "Join group" in Step 1 first, and verify that the Google account logged into your browser matches the Google account active in your Google Play Store app.

How can I help Factictionary graduate to public release on Google Play?

Keep the app installed for at least 14 days and open a couple of your favorite tools every few days. Your feedback helps us polish the experience for everyone!

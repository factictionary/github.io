About Factictionary

Factictionary is a privacy-first web platform dedicated to delivering high-demand browser utilities (PDF, Image, Video, Audio, and Developer tools) with 100% on-device processing. Hosted on GitHub Pages, we ensure your files never touch an external server.

Our Privacy-First Architecture

Traditional cloud converters require you to upload confidential documents and photos to remote servers. Factictionary leverages WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Audio APIs to process your files locally right inside your web browser. Zero bytes uploaded, zero data collection.

Community & Open Support

All utilities on Factictionary are completely free and open to everyone worldwide. We rely on community donations (via Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors, and Crypto/UPI) to maintain and build new high-demand utilities. Thank you for supporting open web privacy!

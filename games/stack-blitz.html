About this app

How high can you build before it all comes crashing down? Stack Blitz is a fast-paced block-stacking game that tests your timing, focus, and nerve. Tap to drop each moving block onto the stack below. Land it perfectly and your tower grows. Miss — and it's over.

What you'll love

  • Simple one-tap controls — anyone can play, few can master
  • Ten unique worlds with distinct visual styles and music
  • Endless mode for high-score chasers
  • Smooth 3D graphics that run on any device
  • Quick sessions perfect for breaks
  • No internet required — play anywhere
